Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following Android games (the “Games”):

  • Neon Night Pursuit (com.orqa.neonnightpursuit)
  • Desert Rally Raid (com.orqa.desertrallyraid)
  • Skyline Grand Prix (com.orqa.skylinegp)

Summary

The Games do not collect, store, or share any personal data. They work fully offline.

Data we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data.
  • No gameplay analytics or usage statistics.
  • No crash reports sent to us.

On-device storage

Level progress and coin totals are saved only in the app's private storage on your own device so the game can remember which levels you have unlocked. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the Game.

Advertising and purchases

The Games contain no third-party advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs.

Children

Because the Games collect no personal data from anyone, there is no personal data from children to collect, use, or share.

Permissions

The Games request no sensitive Android permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address before the change takes effect in a new release of the Games.
